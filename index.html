How Ember works

Ember estimates how much attention a story will end up deserving, then subtracts what looks like noise rather than interest. Scores are absolute and logarithmic: the same number means the same thing in every timeframe. Before penalties, each +1 is an e-fold increase in the weighted reception index; when points and comments grow together, that is roughly 1.95× both.

Ember=magnitude−heat−staleness

Where

magnitude= ln(pointsmaturity+1) +0.5⋅ ln(commentsmaturity+1) heat= emberWeight⋅damping⋅ max(0, ln(ratioheat threshold)) staleness=0.25⋅ max(0, ln(age24))
maturity= max(1− e−ageemberMaturity ,0.2) ratio= comments+1points+1 heat threshold=emberHeat⋅ type factor type factor=1.75 for Ask HN,  1 otherwise damping= max( emberEndorsementemberEndorsement+points ,0.5)

Names in orange are the parameters you can override. Everything else is either measured from the story or computed above.

Terms

magnitude
Size of the reception, with comments counting half as much as points.
heat
What gets subtracted for arguing.
staleness
A slow decay that starts after 24 hours.
maturity
How much of its final reception the story has likely collected. Dividing by it projects a young story forward, so a breakout surfaces hours earlier instead of losing on raw counts. Floored at 0.2 so a minutes-old story cannot explode.
ratio
Comments per point. Past the heat threshold it starts to indicate argument rather than depth.
heat threshold
The expected discussion ratio. Ask HN uses a 1.75× baseline because participation is the format rather than evidence of a pile-on.
damping
A high ratio only means pile-on while few people have endorsed the story. Once many have, the same ratio describes a widely approved but divisive piece, so the penalty shrinks. Floored at 0.5 so endorsement can forgive at most half the penalty and extra heat still lowers the score.
points, comments
What the story has collected so far.
age
Hours since it was posted.

Parameters

Accumulation time constant in hours. Higher projects young stories further forward.

Base ratio at which comments start counting against a story. Ask HN gets a higher participatory baseline.

How hard the heat penalty bites. The minimum preserves a declining score after the threshold.

Points at which endorsement reaches its maximum half-penalty forgiveness.

Score needed for the don't miss badge. Does not affect ranking.
